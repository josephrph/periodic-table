What I need from you — six steps
  1. Hold the iPad in portrait. Not landscape. Portrait is where the problems were reported, and it is the narrower case.
  2. Scroll all the way to the bottom. This page is several screens long. Everything below matters.
  3. Take 5 screenshots as you scroll: one of the capability table in section 1, then one of each of the four component cards in section 2. Overlapping shots are fine — too many is better than too few.
  4. For each of the four components, tell me which lettered variant looks best. Just the letter: “A”, “B”, and so on. If none of them look right, say so and describe what is wrong — your eyes on the real device beat my numbers.
  5. Tap “Copy all results” at the very bottom and paste the text to me. This is the single most useful thing on the page: it carries the exact measurements.
  6. That is everything. No other taps, no typing, no sign-in. Nothing is saved and nothing is sent anywhere — the page only measures and displays.

If the text in step 5 will not copy, screenshot the box above the button instead. Landscape is not needed unless I ask for it later.
